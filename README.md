# Pizzeria Italy — End-to-End Business Analytics Dashboard (Power BI)
Interactive Power BI analytics solution developed for **Pizzeria Italy** (New Jersey, USA) as part of the *Business Analytics & Data Culture* course in the **Master's in Data Analytics and Decision Support Systems** at Coimbra Business School | ISCAC.
The project translates raw operational and transactional data into actionable insights for the restaurant general manager, optimizing revenue, operational capacity, and menu engineering.

## Business Questions Addressed
* **Sales & Revenue Performance:** Total revenue trends, average order value (AOV), and seasonal demand fluctuations.
* **Operational Peaks & Capacity Utilization:** Peak order hours and days, table turn rate, and seating occupancy (evaluating full capacity across 15 tables and 60 seats).
* **Menu Engineering & Product Mix:** Identification of best-sellers and underperforming products, sales by pizza category and size, and menu rationalization opportunities.

## Technical Architecture & Features
* **ETL & Data Modeling:** Robust Power Query transformation pipelines automated to handle dynamic CSV ingestion, structured into an optimized Star Schema.
* **DAX Measures:** Advanced calculations for revenue, average ticket, table occupancy rates, and time intelligence comparisons (YoY/MoM).
* **Storytelling & Visual Design:** Multi-page executive dashboard featuring custom visuals, AI-driven visual analytics, synchronized slicers, and custom tooltip pages for drill-through exploration.

## Stack & Tools
* **BI Platform:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Language & Queries:** DAX (Data Analysis Expressions), M (Power Query)
* **Data Sources:** Transactional CSV extracts (orders, items, pizza types, and operational metrics)
